Publishing Checklist

  • 1 Title — Write a clear, compelling headline that describes the post.
  • 2 Slug — Auto-generated from title. Keep it short for better SEO.
  • 3 Category — Assign to a relevant category so readers can find it.
  • 4 Short Description — A 1–2 sentence summary shown on listing cards.
  • 5 Featured Image — Upload a high-quality image (16:9 ratio recommended).
  • 6 Body — Write your full post content here.
  • 7 Is Featured — Check this to show the post in the homepage hero section.
💡

Writing Tips

  • ✦ Aim for a title under 70 characters for best display on cards and browser tabs.
  • ✦ The short description is used as a preview — make it engaging to drive clicks.
  • ✦ Use a 16:9 featured image for the best hero and card display.
  • ✦ Only one post can be shown as the hero at a time — use "Is Featured" wisely.
